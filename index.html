About HunarGah

A skills institute built for Gilgit-Baltistan, working for clients everywhere

HunarGah is a registered skill and professional development institute headquartered in Gilgit, Gilgit-Baltistan. It exists to help local youth learn how to earn from the digital economy — training them in real, market-ready skills and connecting that talent to clients around the world.

Alongside training, the HunarGah team takes on client work directly — from ad campaigns and content to full websites and bookkeeping — for businesses that need reliable, accountable digital support. The team also runs digital-awareness sessions and workshops across the region.
